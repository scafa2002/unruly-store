Delivery and returns

How does ordering work?

Place your order online and we call you to confirm the address before dispatch. You can pay on delivery or send the order on WhatsApp.

Can I track my order?

Yes. Sign in to your account to see every order and its status, from pending to delivered.

What if my size is sold out?

Sold-out sizes are crossed out on the product page. Join the email list and we will tell you about the next drop.
